How it works

Intent in. Policy in the middle. Money last.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Read the instruction, the balances, and what is actually due.

  2. 02

    Check rules

    Limits, new payees, duplicates, reserve floor, unusual activity.

  3. 03

    Act or ask

    Allowed work runs. Everything else waits in the queue.

  4. 04

    Record why

    Every allow, block, delay, or request is explained.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Does it move money on its own?

Only inside your rules. Over a limit, a new recipient, or anything unusual waits for you.

Where does Binance fit?

Binance is the financial infrastructure. On-chain wallets are used only when that is the right tool.

Is this a chat app?

No. The product is an operator queue — decisions, explanations, and a policy firewall.

How it works

Intent in. Policy in the middle. Money last.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Parse the instruction against live balances, due dates, and open commitments.

  2. 02

    Check rules

    Limits, new payees, duplicates, reserve floor, unusual patterns — before anything moves.

  3. 03

    Act or ask

    Allowed work settles. Everything else waits in the operator queue with a reason.

  4. 04

    Record why

    Every allow, block, delay, or request is logged — auditable, not opaque.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Does it move money on its own?

Only inside your rules. Over a limit, a new recipient, or anything unusual waits in the queue for you.

Where does Binance fit?

Binance is the primary financial rail. On-chain wallets are used only when settlement requires them.
